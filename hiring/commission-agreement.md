# Community Liaison Commission Agreement
**Better Days Home Health**

---

This Community Liaison Commission Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the date signed below, between:

**Better Days Home Health**, operated by Curran Capital LLC, a Virginia limited liability company, located in Hampton Roads, Virginia ("Company"), and

**[Liaison Full Name]**, an independent contractor residing at [Address] ("Liaison").

---

## 1. Relationship of the Parties

Liaison is engaged as an independent contractor and not as an employee of the Company. Liaison is responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes on compensation received under this Agreement. The Company will not withhold taxes, provide benefits, or offer workers' compensation coverage. Liaison will receive a Form 1099 at year end.

---

## 2. Scope of Work

Liaison agrees to:

- Build and maintain referral relationships with hospital discharge planners, social workers, skilled nursing facilities, assisted living facilities, and physician offices in the assigned territory
- Submit referrals through the Company's CRM system (HomeHealthHub)
- Log all outreach activity weekly in the CRM
- Represent Better Days Home Health in a professional manner at all times

---

## 3. Territory

Liaison's primary territory is: **[Virginia Beach / Chesapeake | Norfolk / Portsmouth | Hampton / Newport News]** (select one at signing).

The Company reserves the right to assign additional or overlapping territories at its discretion.

---

## 4. Compensation

### 4a. Ramp Stipend
The Company will pay Liaison **$500 per month for the first 90 days** of this Agreement to cover gas and business development expenses. This stipend is paid regardless of admission volume and ends automatically at day 90. No repayment is required if the Liaison does not meet volume thresholds.

### 4b. Admission Commission
The Company will pay Liaison **$200 per admitted case** originating from a referral submitted by Liaison. A case is considered admitted when a patient is accepted, scheduled, and services begin.

### 4c. 90-Day Retention Bonus
The Company will pay Liaison a **$100 bonus per case** that remains active and receiving services at 90 days from the admission date.

### 4d. Monthly Continuation Payment
The Company will pay Liaison **$50 per month for each active case** that has been receiving services for more than 90 days, as long as the case remains open. This payment is made monthly alongside the regular commission payment.

### 4e. Monthly Retainer
In any calendar month where Liaison generates **5 or more admitted cases**, the Company will pay Liaison an additional **$600 retainer** for that month. The retainer is paid monthly and does not carry over. Volume must be maintained each month for the retainer to apply.

---

## 5. Payment Schedule

All commissions, bonuses, continuation payments, and retainers are calculated at the end of each calendar month and paid within **15 days of month end** via [check / direct deposit — select at signing].

---

## 6. Term and Termination

This Agreement begins on the date signed and continues on a month-to-month basis. Either party may terminate this Agreement with **14 days written notice**. Upon termination:

- The ramp stipend stops immediately
- Commissions already earned on admitted cases are paid out at the next payment date
- 90-day bonuses and continuation payments on active cases continue to be paid as long as those cases remain open, up to 90 days post-termination

---

## 7. Confidentiality

Liaison agrees to keep confidential all patient information, referral source relationships, pricing, and internal business information of the Company. This obligation survives termination of the Agreement. All patient information is governed by HIPAA and applicable Virginia law.

---

## 8. Non-Solicitation

For a period of **12 months** following termination, Liaison agrees not to solicit the Company's referral sources, employees, or active patients on behalf of a competing home health agency operating in Hampton Roads.

---

## 9. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter herein and supersedes all prior discussions or agreements. Any modifications must be made in writing and signed by both parties.
