Better Days Home Health
Community Liaison Commission Agreement
Independent Contractor Agreement

This Community Liaison Commission Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of ________________, 2026, between Better Days Home Health, LLC, a Virginia limited liability company located in Hampton Roads, Virginia ("Company"), and ________________________________, an independent contractor residing at ________________________________ ("Liaison").

1. Relationship of the Parties

Liaison is engaged as an independent contractor and not as an employee of the Company. Liaison is responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes on compensation received under this Agreement. The Company will not withhold taxes, provide benefits, or offer workers' compensation coverage. Liaison will receive a Form 1099 at year end.

2. Scope of Work

Liaison agrees to:

  • Build and maintain referral relationships with hospital discharge planners, social workers, skilled nursing facilities, assisted living facilities, and physician offices in the assigned territory
  • Submit all referrals through the Company's referral tracking system
  • Log all outreach activity weekly in the tracking system
  • Represent Better Days Home Health in a professional manner at all times
3. Territory

Liaison's primary territory is (select one at signing):

Virginia Beach / Chesapeake
Norfolk / Portsmouth
Hampton / Newport News

The Company reserves the right to assign additional or overlapping territories at its discretion.

4. Compensation
4a. Ramp Stipend

The Company will pay Liaison $500 per month for the first 90 days of this Agreement to cover gas and business development expenses. This stipend ends automatically at day 90. No repayment is required.

The stipend is contingent on Liaison making a good-faith effort to build referral relationships, including regular outreach activity logged in the Company's tracking system. If Liaison demonstrates no meaningful activity or performance during the ramp period, the Company reserves the right to terminate this Agreement immediately and cease the stipend without further obligation.

4b. Admission Commission

The Company will pay Liaison $200 per admitted case originating from a referral submitted by Liaison. A case is considered admitted when a patient is accepted, scheduled, and services begin.

4c. 90-Day Retention Bonus

The Company will pay Liaison a one-time $100 bonus per case that remains active and receiving services at 90 days from the admission date.

4d. Monthly Continuation Payment

The Company will pay Liaison $50 per month for each active case that has been receiving services for more than 90 days, for as long as the case remains open.

4e. Monthly Retainer

In any calendar month where Liaison generates 5 or more admitted cases, the Company will pay an additional $600 retainer for that month. Volume must be maintained each month for the retainer to apply.

5. Payment Schedule

All compensation is calculated at the end of each calendar month and paid within 15 days of month end via:

Check
Direct deposit
6. Term and Termination

This Agreement begins on the date signed and continues month to month. Either party may terminate with 14 days written notice. Upon termination, the ramp stipend stops immediately. Commissions already earned are paid at the next payment date. Continuation payments on active cases continue for up to 90 days post-termination.

7. Confidentiality

Liaison agrees to keep confidential all patient information, referral source relationships, pricing, and internal business information of the Company. This obligation survives termination. All patient information is governed by HIPAA and applicable Virginia law.

8. Non-Solicitation

For 12 months following termination, Liaison agrees not to solicit the Company's referral sources, employees, or active patients on behalf of a competing home health agency operating in Hampton Roads.

9. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions. Any modifications must be in writing and signed by both parties.
