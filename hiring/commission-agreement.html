Community Liaison Commission Agreement
Independent Contractor Agreement
This Community Liaison Commission Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of , 2026, between Better Days Home Health, LLC, a Virginia limited liability company located in Hampton Roads, Virginia ("Company"), and , an independent contractor residing at ("Liaison").
1. Relationship of the Parties

Liaison is engaged as an independent contractor and not as an employee of the Company. Liaison is solely responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes on compensation received under this Agreement. The Company will not withhold taxes, provide employee benefits, or offer workers' compensation coverage. Liaison will receive a Form 1099-NEC at year end for all compensation paid.

2. Scope of Work

Liaison agrees to perform the following services on behalf of the Company:

  • Build and maintain referral relationships with hospital discharge planners, social workers, skilled nursing facilities, assisted living facilities, and physician offices within the assigned territory
  • Submit all patient referrals through the Company's designated referral tracking system
  • Log all outreach and business development activity weekly in the tracking system
  • Represent Better Days Home Health, LLC in a professional and ethical manner at all times
  • Comply with all applicable HIPAA regulations in the handling of patient information
3. Territory

Liaison's primary territory is (select one at signing):

Virginia Beach / Chesapeake
Norfolk / Portsmouth
Hampton / Newport News

The Company reserves the right to assign additional or overlapping territories at its discretion with reasonable notice to Liaison.

4. Compensation
Component Amount Condition
Ramp Stipend $500 / month First 90 days only. Contingent on good-faith activity. See Section 4a.
Admission Commission $200 / case Paid when patient is admitted and services begin.
90-Day Bonus $100 / case One-time bonus per case still active at 90 days.
Monthly Continuation $50 / case / month Paid monthly for each active case beyond 90 days.
Monthly Retainer $600 / month Earned in any month with 5 or more admitted cases.
4a. Ramp Stipend — Performance Requirement

The $500 monthly ramp stipend is contingent on Liaison making a good-faith effort to build referral relationships, including regular outreach activity logged in the Company's tracking system. If Liaison demonstrates no meaningful activity or performance during the ramp period, the Company reserves the right to terminate this Agreement immediately and cease the stipend without further obligation. No repayment of stipend already paid is required.

4b. No Cap on Earnings

There is no maximum limit on commissions, bonuses, continuation payments, or retainers earned under this Agreement.

5. Payment Schedule

All compensation is calculated at the end of each calendar month and paid within 15 days of month end via:

Check mailed to Liaison's address on file
Direct deposit — banking information provided at signing
6. Term and Termination

This Agreement begins on the date signed below and continues on a month-to-month basis until terminated. Either party may terminate this Agreement with 14 days written notice. The Company reserves the right to terminate immediately and without notice if Liaison engages in misconduct, misrepresentation, HIPAA violations, or fails to demonstrate any meaningful performance during the ramp period.

Upon termination: the ramp stipend ceases immediately; commissions already earned on admitted cases are paid at the next scheduled payment date; 90-day bonuses and monthly continuation payments on active cases continue to be paid for up to 90 days post-termination as long as those cases remain open.

7. Confidentiality

Liaison agrees to hold in strict confidence all patient information, referral source relationships, business processes, pricing, and proprietary information of the Company, both during and after the term of this Agreement. Liaison shall not disclose any such information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Company. All patient information is governed by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and applicable Virginia law. This obligation survives termination of this Agreement indefinitely.

8. Non-Solicitation

For a period of 12 months following the termination of this Agreement, Liaison agrees not to directly or indirectly solicit, contact, or accept referrals from the Company's referral sources, recruit the Company's employees, or solicit the Company's active patients, on behalf of any competing home health agency operating in Hampton Roads, Virginia.

9. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia. Any disputes arising under this Agreement shall be resolved in the courts of Hampton Roads, Virginia.

10. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter herein and supersedes all prior discussions, representations, and agreements. Any modification to this Agreement must be made in writing and signed by both parties to be effective.
